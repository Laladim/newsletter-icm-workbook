# Source contract for Beyond Banks

Source: the approved `publication-brief.md`.

## Approved sources

- Public notices, filings, and opinions may establish the event they directly
  record.
- Reputable public reporting may supply independent context when its own sources
  and limitations are visible.
- Interested statements may document what a subject claims, but do not provide
  independent corroboration.

This sanitized example contains no private source route and no credential.

## Source hierarchy

1. Direct public authority or record.
2. Independent public reporting or analysis with traceable support.
3. Interested public material, labeled for what it is.
4. Community or anecdotal material used only as a lead for verification.
5. Anonymous rumor, unattributed generated text, and inaccessible claims are
   disallowed as evidence.

## Required checks

- Open the canonical location and verify the date and identity.
- Check freshness, duplication, and prior coverage.
- Anchor the main event in a direct public record when available.
- Seek independent context for a material interpretation.
- Verify numbers, quotations, status, and attribution exactly.
- Route allegations, legal conclusions, or individualized advice to qualified
  review or omit them.
- Qualify or omit evidence outside the approved low-risk tolerance.

## Citation and evidence record

Record source title, owner, type, date, location, access time, supported claim,
independence, and limits. Missing, contradictory, stale, or inaccessible
evidence cannot be hidden by confident prose.
