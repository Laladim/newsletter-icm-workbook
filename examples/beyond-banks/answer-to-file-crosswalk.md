# Answer-to-file crosswalk

This crosswalk shows where approved discovery answers land. It does not add new
facts or decisions.

| Discovery answer | Approved source location | Generated destination | Why it belongs there |
|---|---|---|---|
| Audience and reader problem | publication brief, Audience and recurring problem | `CONTEXT.md` and `editorial-contract.md` | route every edition toward one reader outcome |
| Editorial thesis and promise | publication brief, Editorial thesis, promise, and positioning | `CONTEXT.md` and `editorial-contract.md` | control selection, drafting, and quality |
| Topic boundaries and point of view | publication brief, Topic boundaries and edition shape | `editorial-contract.md` | define what leads, supports, waits, or stays out |
| Evidence hierarchy and risk | publication brief, Evidence and risk | `source-contract.md` | govern research and release blockers |
| Voice and expertise posture | publication brief, Voice, expertise, and visuals | `editorial-contract.md` | prevent invented authority or advice |
| Visual requirement | publication brief, Voice, expertise, and visuals | `visual-contract.md` | make visual use optional and inspectable |
| Cadence and intended reader action | publication brief, Topic boundaries and edition shape | `editorial-contract.md` | shape the reader experience without granting send authority |
| Output, review, and platform intent | publication brief, Cadence, ownership, delivery, and metrics | `delivery-contract.md` and `CONTEXT.md` | separate local review from future integrations |
| Human authority | publication brief, Human decisions | `CONTEXT.md` and `delivery-contract.md` | keep schedule, send, activate, and publish human-only |
| Capability needs | all approved brief sections | `skill-bindings.md` | bind only verified procedures needed by a stage |
| Brief approval | publication brief front matter | `CONTEXT.md` brief hash | prevent silent drift from approved decisions |
