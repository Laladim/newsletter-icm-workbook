# Editorial contract for {{publication_title}}

## Reader and promise

Define the primary reader, the decision or problem this publication supports,
and what readers can reliably expect from each edition.

## Voice and point of view

Define tone, expertise level, allowed opinion, prohibited postures, preferred
language, and examples of work that sounds right. Do not describe a model's
personality in place of the publication's identity.

## Edition anatomy

Define required and optional sections, approximate length, citation style,
links, calls to action, and any recurring modules.

## Editorial selection

Define what deserves a lead, what belongs as a supporting item, what is out of
scope, and how unused material is routed.

## Quality bar

Define the observable qualities of a strong edition and the failures that block
release. Put reusable execution procedures in skills rather than copying them
here.
